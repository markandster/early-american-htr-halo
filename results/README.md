# Public Competition Benchmark Results

Development tuning, ablations, and checkpoint selection run exclusively on **`val`** (`CC-BY-4.0` Revolutionary City in-collection balanced split across all four institutions: `train=2,897` pages / `92.1%` across `17` large volumes [`1,424` APS / `1,303` HSP / `124` LCP / `46` MSS], `val=126` pages / `4.0%` across `21` groups [`85` APS / `31` HSP / `4` LCP / `6` MSS], and frozen `test_public=122` pages / `3.9%` across `21` groups [`92` APS / `10` HSP / `16` LCP / `4` MSS]; hard-disjoint by item/collection group `max_id_gap=30` + `REVCITY_COLLECTION_OVERRIDES`, `writer_key`, image SHA-256, `>= 8`-word exact text, and 32-word n-grams, with advisory perceptual `dHash` review). Split shape & reporting limits: all `42` small groups (`<= 40` pages, `248` pages total) go to eval (`val=4.0%`, `test_public=3.9%`, falling short of `5%` because small groups are exhausted) while `train` consists of `17` large volumes; default RevCity metadata has `attributed_hands = 0` unless `PORTAL_CREATOR_MAP_PATH` is supplied (so splits are **document-group disjoint** rather than writer-attributed or collection-verified across all collections); per-institution numbers outside APS (`HSP test=10`, `LCP val=4`, `MSS val=6 / test=4`) are **not reportable** as standalone per-institution benchmarks; and `hard_case_held_out.jsonl` contains `8` sparse (`<= 7`-word) diagnostic pages. Frozen **`test_public`** numbers below are populated only by a single post-freeze evaluation command after checkpoint, decoding settings, and metrics are locked. **LOC *By the People*** (`LCCN 2020446971`, Public Domain) is reported separately as an out-of-collection genre-shift challenge panel.

Every reported evaluation records:
- **Strict & Lenient WER / CER:** Strict preserves case, punctuation, period spelling, and line breaks (`Unicode NFC`); lenient strips punctuation and lowercases; zero-shot baselines also report dual-convention `raw_strict_cer_pct` against uncleaned XML.
- **Line-Break & Order Metrics:** `newline_cer_pct`, `line_boundary_f1_pct`, and `order_tolerant_cer_pct` (`eahtr.metrics.score_line_breaks`).
- **Causal & Statistical Controls:** Matched-compute Halo sham control (full-mode Colab `sham_short`, capped at `<= 512` pages, is a short unmatched control; smoke mode matches `real` at `128`), same-checkpoint **image-swap delta** (`swap_cer_degradation`), and clustered 95% **paired bootstrap CIs** (`paired_bootstrap_ci` by `document_id`, weighted by normalized `ref_chars` with pre-inference unique 1-to-1 baseline `record_id` matching).
- **Decoding Health Denominators:** Separately reports `loop_pages`, `budget_capped_pages`, `non_terminated_pages`, `editorial_leaks`, `modernizations`, `thinking_leak_pages`, and mean `eahtr.rewards`.
